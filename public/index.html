FAQ

Common questions

What kind of projects do you take on?

Full-stack web applications, SaaS platforms, AI chatbots and RAG systems, LLM integrations (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini), e-commerce stores, and DevOps/QA automation. I work on freelance projects, contracts, and full-time remote roles.

Can you add AI features to my existing product?

Yes — this is my most requested engagement. I integrate AI chatbots, document Q&A (RAG), smart search, and content generation into existing products without rewriting your stack, whether it's React, Angular, PHP, or WordPress.

How quickly can you start, and how fast do you respond?

I typically start new projects within a week and respond to every inquiry within 24 hours. For urgent fixes or consultations, same-day availability is often possible.

How do you charge for projects?

Fixed price for well-defined scopes, hourly or monthly retainer for ongoing work. Every project starts with a free discovery call, after which you get a clear proposal with milestones — no surprises.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. I offer maintenance retainers covering monitoring, bug fixes, performance optimisation, and new feature iterations — most of my client relationships span years, not weeks.

Which time zones do you work with?

I'm based in India (IST) and collaborate daily with clients in the US, UK, Europe, and Israel. I keep generous overlap hours and communicate async-first, so time zones are never a blocker.
